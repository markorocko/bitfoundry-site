About Bit Foundry

Building AI you can actually trust.

AI is reshaping how people find answers — but a single model, however powerful, is still one opinion that can be confidently wrong. We think the answer is consensus.

Bit Foundry is a California software company building the tools for this new era: products where the world's most powerful AI models work together — cross-checked, adjudicated, and accountable — so the answer you act on is one you can trust.

Our thesis

An ensemble of elite models beats any single model — every time.

It's true in machine learning, and it's true for the answers you rely on. Rather than betting a decision on one model's blind spot, we run the best models in parallel, surface where they agree and disagree, and let a top model adjudicate a single, sourced verdict. That principle runs through everything we build.
