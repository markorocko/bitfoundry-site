About

The company behind the answer.

Bit Foundry Labs builds Gangsta AI and Ask Consensus, which put the world's top AI models side by side so you can see where they agree, where they split, and make the call yourself. Agreement is a signal, not a verdict. We show you the evidence; you decide.

Bit Foundry Labs is the product studio of Bit Foundry, a California S-corporation.

Our thesis

Compare answers. See where they differ.

Multiple model responses can reveal useful differences, but consensus can still be wrong. Our products help people inspect AI outputs and make their own judgments.
